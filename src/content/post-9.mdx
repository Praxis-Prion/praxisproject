Now I check it. Frequently. Too frequently.

look at soil
look closer at soil
gently poke soil
immediately regret poking soil

did I just interrupt something

day three (suspicion)

Still nothing. This feels personal now. I planted multiple seeds. Statistically, something should have happened.

maybe they’re shy

maybe they’re gone

<a href={testImage.src} target="_blank"> <Image src={testImage} alt="nothing yet" class="float-left" /> </a>

I consider digging one up. Just to check. Just to confirm existence. I do not do this. I want to, which is worse.

<a href={testImage.src} target="_blank"> <Image src={testImage} alt="restraint" class="float-right" /> </a>
plant seed
doubt seed
question soil
breakthrough (maybe)

A small green thing appears. Tiny. Fragile. Possibly a plant. Possibly something else. I stare at it like I had something to do with this.

it lives
it grows
it might survive

this changes everything

escalation

Now I care too much. Watering schedules. Sunlight tracking. Soil moisture analysis. I am monitoring this one small plant like it’s a critical system.

grow faster

please

acceptance (temporary)

Eventually I understand: it grows when it grows. Not when I check. Not when I worry. Not when I stand there staring at it.

conclusion (ongoing)

The garden is quiet. Slow. Unbothered by my expectations.

And yet—

I will check it again in five minutes.
